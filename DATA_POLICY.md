# Data Policy

Civilysta is a public utility for civic engagement. This document describes how data is handled on the platform.

---

## What's Public

**Cause pages are public by design.** The entire point of a cause page is to be shared, linked, indexed, and discovered. Cause titles, descriptions, AI-generated content, hero images, supporter counts, and comments are all publicly accessible. This is intentional — civic organizing requires visibility.

---

## What's Private

**Supporter email addresses are private.** When someone supports a cause by entering their email, that address is visible only to the cause creator. Civilysta will never:

- Sell supporter email addresses to any third party
- Share supporter lists with political campaigns, marketers, or commercial interests
- Use supporter emails for any purpose other than connecting them to the cause they signed up for

Cause creators are expected to respect their supporters' trust. Using petition email addresses for spam or commercial purposes violates the spirit of this platform.

---

## What We Don't Collect

- No tracking cookies beyond what's required for authentication
- No behavioral analytics or user profiling
- No demographic data on supporters (we store email and optional name — nothing else)

---

## Aggregate Data

Over time, Civilysta may publish anonymized, aggregate data about civic engagement trends — for example, what types of issues are most common, which regions have the most active organizing, or how cause engagement changes over time.

This data will be:
- **Fully anonymized** — no individual emails, names, or identifiable information
- **Published openly** — available to researchers, journalists, and the public
- **Never sold commercially** — aggregate data is a public good, not a revenue stream

---

## Open Source Guarantee

Civilysta's source code is licensed under [AGPL-3.0](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.en.html). This means:

- The code is always open and inspectable
- Anyone can self-host their own instance
- If the platform ever shuts down, cause creators will be given the opportunity to export their data before closure

---

## Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is a **civic data commons** — an open, queryable dataset of civic engagement activity that's useful to researchers, journalists, local government, and advocacy organizations. This will only happen with explicit, informed consent from platform participants, and only with data that's already public (cause pages, supporter counts, issue categories).

Private data (supporter emails, authentication details) will never be part of any open dataset.

---

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the platform grows. Material changes — especially anything affecting supporter email privacy — will be communicated clearly and in advance. The source of truth is always this file in the repository.
